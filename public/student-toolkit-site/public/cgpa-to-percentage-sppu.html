SPPU CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) converts CGPA to percentage using a grade-band formula from Circular No. 332 of 2020, applicable to the CBCS scheme from 2019-20 onwards. The formula depends on which grade band your CGPA falls into.

Formula

CGPAGradePercentage
9.50 – 10.00O20 × CGPA − 100
8.25 – 9.49A+12 × CGPA − 25
6.75 – 8.24A10 × CGPA − 7.5
5.75 – 6.74B+5 × CGPA + 26.25
5.25 – 5.74B10 × CGPA − 2.5
4.75 – 5.24C10 × CGPA − 2.5
4.00 – 4.74D6.6 × CGPA + 13.6

Example

CGPA 8.00 falls in grade A, so percentage = 10 × 8.00 − 7.5 = 72.5%.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SPPU formula the same for every course?

Not always. Some architecture programmes and older schemes use different rules, so check the circular for your scheme.

Can I use this for SGPA too?

Sources commonly apply the same grade-band formula to SGPA, but your official mark sheet is the final authority.
