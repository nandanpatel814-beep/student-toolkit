VTU CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) commonly uses a simple deduction formula to convert CGPA or SGPA into a percentage.

Formula

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

Example

CGPA 8.00 gives (8.00 − 0.75) × 10 = 72.5%.

Frequently asked questions

Does this apply to every VTU scheme?

Widely published sources use this formula, but confirm with your scheme and the official notification.

How do I convert percentage back to CGPA?

Use CGPA = percentage ÷ 10 + 0.75, or choose the Percentage → CGPA option in the calculator.
