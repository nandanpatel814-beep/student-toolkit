Mumbai University CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Mumbai University uses different rules by faculty. Engineering uses (7.1 × CGPI) + 11, while several other faculties use (CGPI − 0.75) × 10. Choose the scheme printed on your own transcript.

Formulas

FacultyFormula
Engineering7.1 × CGPI + 11
Other faculties(CGPI − 0.75) × 10

Example

Engineering CGPI 8.00 gives 7.1 × 8 + 11 = 67.8%. For a faculty using the deduction formula, 8.00 gives 72.5%.

Frequently asked questions

Which formula should I use?

Use the one that matches your faculty and the scheme on your marksheet. Using a single formula for everything can give a percentage that does not match your transcript.

Is this result official?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Ask the university for an official equivalence certificate if required.
